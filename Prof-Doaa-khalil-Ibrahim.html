Biography

Prof. Doaa Khalil Ibrahim (IEEE M'06, SM'13) was born in Egypt in December 1973. She received M.Sc. and Ph.D. degrees in digital protection from Cairo University in 2001 and 2005. She joined Cairo University as Demonstrator in 1996, becoming Professor in December 2016. She contributes to World Bank and Egyptian Ministry of Higher Education accreditation programs. Her research spans digital protection, microgrids, renewable energy, and smart grid systems.
